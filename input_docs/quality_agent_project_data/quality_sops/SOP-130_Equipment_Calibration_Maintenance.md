# SOP-130: Equipment Calibration and Maintenance

**ISO 9001:2015 Reference:** Clause 7.1.5 – Monitoring and Measuring Resources

## Purpose
Ensures that measurement and process equipment remains within specified accuracy and performance limits, and that maintenance is performed on schedule to prevent quality escapes.

## Scope
Applies to all metrology tools, process equipment with measurement functions, and any tool listed on the site Calibration Master List.

## Procedure
1. **Calibration Schedule**: Every applicable tool must have a defined calibration interval based on manufacturer recommendation and criticality (typically 30, 90, or 180 days). Intervals may not be extended without Quality Engineering approval.
2. **Calibration Execution**: Calibration must be performed by qualified personnel using standards traceable to a national metrology institute. Results must be recorded, including as-found and as-left values.
3. **Out-of-Tolerance Handling**: If a tool is found out of tolerance, all product measured on that tool since the last successful calibration must be evaluated for potential impact, and a non-conformance record opened per SOP-101 if affected product was released.
4. **Preventive Maintenance**: Preventive maintenance (PM) tasks must be completed within the scheduled window. A PM overdue by more than 5 business days is escalated to the Equipment Owner and Quality as a compliance risk.
5. **Drift Monitoring**: Tools exhibiting a pattern of measurement drift across two or more consecutive calibration cycles must be flagged for engineering review, even if currently within tolerance.
6. **Records Retention**: Calibration and PM records must be retained for a minimum of 3 years or per customer contractual requirements, whichever is longer.
