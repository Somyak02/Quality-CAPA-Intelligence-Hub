# SOP-175: Document Control and Change Management

**ISO 9001:2015 Reference:** Clause 7.5 – Documented Information

## Purpose
Ensures that all controlled documents (SOPs, work instructions, quality manuals) are current, approved, and accessible to the personnel who need them, and that changes are properly authorized.

## Scope
Applies to all controlled documents used in production, quality, and engineering processes.

## Procedure
1. **Document Approval**: All new or revised controlled documents must be approved by the designated document owner and Quality representative before release. No document may be used on the production floor prior to formal approval.
2. **Revision Control**: Each document must display a unique revision number and effective date. Floor copies must be checked against the master list at a minimum monthly frequency; any mismatch is a reportable finding.
3. **Change Request Process**: Changes to an existing controlled document require a documented change request describing the reason for change, impact assessment, and approval prior to implementation. Emergency temporary changes (e.g., deviations) must still be documented and time-limited.
4. **Distribution and Obsolescence**: Upon approval of a new revision, obsolete copies must be removed from all points of use within 1 business day. Uncontrolled or obsolete copies found in use constitute a non-conformance.
5. **Training on Changes**: Personnel affected by a document change must be trained and records of training completion retained before the new revision becomes effective at their workstation.
6. **Record Retention**: Historical revisions must be archived and retrievable to support audit and traceability requirements, retained for a minimum of 3 years.
