# SOP-145: Contamination Control in Cleanroom Environments

**ISO 9001:2015 Reference:** Clause 8.5.1 – Control of Production and Service Provision

## Purpose
Defines controls to prevent particulate, chemical, and cross-contamination in cleanroom manufacturing areas.

## Scope
Applies to all cleanroom classified areas (Class 100 through Class 10,000) involved in wafer fabrication and sensitive assembly operations.

## Procedure
1. **Environmental Monitoring**: Particle counts, temperature, humidity, and airflow must be continuously monitored. Any excursion beyond the class limit for more than 15 minutes triggers an automatic alarm and incident log entry.
2. **Gowning Compliance**: All personnel must complete gowning certification before cleanroom access. Gowning breaches identified via visual audit or camera review must be logged as a contamination risk event.
3. **HVAC and Filtration**: HEPA/ULPA filter integrity must be tested per the PM schedule (see SOP-130). A confirmed filter fault requires immediate area evaluation for product impact during the fault window.
4. **Material and Tool Segregation**: Trays, carriers, and handling tools must be dedicated to a single product line or product family unless a validated cleaning procedure is completed between uses. Sharing trays without cleaning verification is a direct non-conformance.
5. **Chemical and Slurry Control**: Chemical batches (e.g., CMP slurry, etch chemistries) must be traceable to a batch/lot number, with contamination investigation required if a quality escape is traced to a specific batch.
6. **Incident Response**: Any suspected contamination event must trigger immediate quarantine of potentially affected lots per SOP-101, pending root cause investigation per SOP-122.
