# SOP-101: Non-Conforming Material Handling

**ISO 9001:2015 Reference:** Clause 8.7 – Control of Nonconforming Outputs

## Purpose
This procedure defines the process for identifying, segregating, documenting, and dispositioning material that does not conform to specified requirements, to prevent unintended use or delivery.

## Scope
Applies to all raw material, work-in-process, and finished goods across wafer fabrication, assembly, and packaging operations.

## Procedure
1. **Identification**: Any operator or inspector who identifies a non-conforming condition must immediately apply a red "NON-CONFORMING MATERIAL" hold tag to the affected unit, lot, or container.
2. **Segregation**: Tagged material must be physically moved to a designated quarantine area within 1 shift of identification. Material must never remain in the normal production flow.
3. **System Hold**: The lot status must be updated in the Manufacturing Execution System (MES) to "ON HOLD" within 4 hours of physical tagging. Discrepancy between physical tag status and MES status is itself a reportable non-conformance.
4. **Disposition Review**: A Material Review Board (MRB), consisting of Quality, Engineering, and Production representatives, must review the non-conformance within 3 business days and assign one of the following dispositions: Use-As-Is, Rework, Return-to-Supplier, or Scrap.
5. **Scrap Handling**: Material dispositioned as Scrap must be physically destroyed or defaced to prevent re-entry into production, and stored separately from active production inventory pending destruction.
6. **Closure**: The non-conformance record is closed only after disposition is executed and verified by a Quality representative, with objective evidence attached (photos, rework records, scrap certificates).
