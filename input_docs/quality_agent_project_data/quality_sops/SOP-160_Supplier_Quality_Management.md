# SOP-160: Supplier Quality Management

**ISO 9001:2015 Reference:** Clause 8.4 – Control of Externally Provided Processes, Products and Services

## Purpose
Ensures that materials, components, and services provided by external suppliers meet quality requirements before use in production.

## Scope
Applies to all suppliers providing production materials, chemicals, components, or subcontracted processes.

## Procedure
1. **Supplier Qualification**: New suppliers must complete a documented quality system assessment before the first production shipment is accepted. Shipments received without a completed assessment must be quarantined pending retroactive review.
2. **Incoming Inspection**: Incoming materials are sampled per the defined AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) sampling plan. Certificates of Analysis (CoA) provided by the supplier must be independently verified against a sample of incoming lots at a minimum frequency of 1 in 10.
3. **Non-Conforming Incoming Material**: Any incoming lot failing inspection or CoA verification must be tagged per SOP-101 and a supplier corrective action request (SCAR) issued within 5 business days.
4. **Supplier Scorecarding**: Suppliers are scored quarterly on defect rate, on-time delivery, and CAPA responsiveness. A supplier falling below the minimum score threshold for two consecutive quarters is placed on a formal improvement plan.
5. **Expiration and Shelf Life**: Chemical and material shelf life must be verified at receipt. Material received past its expiration date must be rejected and logged as a non-conformance, regardless of accompanying paperwork.
6. **Periodic Audit**: Critical suppliers must undergo an on-site or remote quality audit at least annually, with findings tracked to closure via CAPA.
